## 线性回归模型的解析解(最小二乘法)

线性模型通过自变量$x$建立了一个参数为$(b,w_1,\cdots,w_d)$的线性函数试图估计$Y$,那么到底应该取什么参数才能使得估计较为合理呢？

设$\{x_{i1},x_{i2},\cdots,x_{id},y_i \} , (i = 1,2,\cdots n)$是一个容量为$n$的样本,令$x_i = (x_{i1},x_{i2},\cdots,x_{id})$.
考虑使用以下函数
$$
L(w,b) = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i = 1}^{n} [y_i - f(x_i)]^2 = \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i = 1}^{n} [y_i - w_1x_{i1} - w_2x_{i2} - \cdots - w_dx_{id} - b]^2
$$
来度量预测值与真实样本之间误差的平方和距离,把上面的函数称为均方误差(Mean-Square Error)。在线性模型当中,均方误差代表了当前参数下所拟合出的直线与样本之间的欧氏距离之和。均方误差越小,代表直线“拟合”的程度越好。
而基于均方误差最小化来进行模型求解的方法被称为最小二乘法(least square method),基于这种思想,我们将$L(w,b)$分布对$b,w_1,\cdots,w_d$求偏导,并令它们等于0,得
$$
\left\{
\begin{align}
&\frac{\partial L}{\partial b} = -2 \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i = 1}^{n} [y_i - w_1x_{i1} - w_2x_{i2} - \cdots - w_dx_{id} - b] = 0
\\
&\frac{\partial  L}{\partial w_j} = -2 \frac{1}{n}\sum_{i = 1}^{n} [y_i - w_1x_{i1} - w_2x_{i2} - \cdots - w_dx_{id} - b]x_{ij} = 0,
\\
&j = 1,2,\cdots, d
\end{align}
\right. \tag{2.1}
$$
化简得
$$
\left\{
\begin{aligned}
n\cdot b + w_1 \sum_{i=1}^n x_{i1} + w_2 \sum_{i=1}^n x_{i2} + \cdots + w_d \sum_{i=1}^n x_{id} &= \sum_{i=1}^n y_i
\\
b \sum_{i=1}^n x_{i1} + w_1 \sum_{i=1}^n x_{i1}^2 + w_2 \sum_{i=1}^n x_{i1} x_{i2} + \cdots + w_d \sum_{i=1}^n x_{i1} x_{ip} &= \sum_{i=1}^n x_{i1} y_i
\\
& \vdots
\\
b \sum_{i=1}^n x_{id} + b_1 \sum_{i=1}^n x_{id} x_{i1} + w_2 \sum_{i=1}^n x_{id} x_{i2} + \cdots + w_d \sum_{i=1}^n x_{id}^2 &= \sum_{i=1}^n x_{id} y_i
\end{aligned}
\right. \tag{2.2}
$$
(2.2)式被称为**正规方程组**,为了求解的方便,将方程组写成矩阵的形式。为此,引入矩阵

$$
X = 
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & x_{11} & x_{12} & \cdots & x_{1d} \\
1 & x_{21} & x_{22} & \cdots & x_{2d} \\
\vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
1 & x_{n1} & x_{n2} & \cdots & x_{nd}
\end{bmatrix}, \quad 
Y = 
\begin{bmatrix}
y_1 \\
y_2 \\
\vdots \\
y_n
\end{bmatrix}, \quad 
W = 
\begin{bmatrix}
b \\
w_1 \\
\vdots \\
w_d
\end{bmatrix}
$$

由于

$$
\begin{align}
&X^T X = 
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 1 & \cdots & 1 \\
x_{11} & x_{21} & \cdots & x_{n1} \\
\vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
x_{1d} & x_{2d} & \cdots & x_{nd}
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & x_{11} & x_{12} & \cdots & x_{1d} \\
1 & x_{21} & x_{22} & \cdots & x_{2d} \\
\vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
1 & x_{n1} & x_{n2} & \cdots & x_{nd}
\end{pmatrix}
=\begin{pmatrix}
n & \sum_{i=1}^n x_{i1} & \cdots & \sum_{i=1}^n x_{id} \\
\sum_{i=1}^n x_{i1} & \sum_{i=1}^n x_{i1}^2 & \cdots & \sum_{i=1}^n x_{i1} x_{id} \\
\vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
\sum_{i=1}^n x_{id} & \sum_{i=1}^n x_{id} x_{i1} & \cdots & \sum_{i=1}^n x_{id}^2
\end{pmatrix} \tag{2.3}
\\
&X^T Y = 
\begin{pmatrix}
1 & 1 & \cdots & 1 \\
x_{11} & x_{21} & \cdots & x_{n1} \\
\vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
x_{1d} & x_{2d} & \cdots & x_{nd}
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}
y_1 \\
y_2 \\
\vdots \\
y_n
\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}
\sum_{i=1}^n y_i \\
\sum_{i=1}^n x_{i1} y_i \\
\vdots \\
\sum_{i=1}^n x_{id} y_i
\end{pmatrix} \tag{2.4}
\end{align}
$$

于是(2.2)式即可写成

$$X^T X W = X^T Y \tag{2.5}$$

这就是正规方程组的矩阵形式。在(2.5)式两边左乘 $X^T X$ 的逆矩阵 $(X^T X)^{-1}$ (设 $(X^T X)^{-1}$ 存在)得到(2.5)的解

$$\hat{W} = 
\begin{pmatrix}
\hat{b} \\
\hat{w}_1 \\
\vdots \\
\hat{w}_d
\end{pmatrix}
= (X^T X)^{-1} X^T Y$$

> - 当$X^TX$不满秩的时候,此时会有多个解析解$\hat w$,它们都能使均方误差最小化。具体选择哪一个解作为输出,将由学习算法的归纳偏好决定,常见的做法是引入正则化项.
> - 如果随机变量$Y$满足正态假设,即$Y_i = \mu(x_i;b,w_1,w_2,\cdots,w_d) + \epsilon_i, \quad \epsilon_i \sim N(0, \sigma^2)$,且各个$\epsilon_i$独立。则其最大似然估计与最小二乘估计的结果相同。
